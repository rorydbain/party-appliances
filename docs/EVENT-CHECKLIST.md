# Event checklist

The core checks cover a local-only Photobooth. Sharing, printing and projection
have their own optional checks.

## Before leaving

- Rehearse Photobooth on the exact Mac that will travel.
- Take at least 20 consecutive flashed photographs and inspect focus and skin
  exposure on a different display.
- Unplug and reconnect the camera once; confirm live view recovers.
- Pack the camera mains adapter or charged spare batteries, two charged flash AA
  sets, data cable, display adapter and extension lead.

If using sharing or receipts:

- Print several receipts, scan their QR codes over mobile data and test paper-out
  recovery.
- Pack spare receipt rolls.
- Copy the event profile and, separately, the private delivery configuration to
  the correct Mac. Never send the delivery file with its upload token publicly.

If using Loop, run it with the full library for several hours and inspect its
diagnostic log.

## At the venue

- Use a stable surface and secure the camera independently of decorative card.
- Mark the tested guest position.
- Check framing on Sidecar, then make one photo with the room lights in their
  final state.
- Confirm the flash pilot light, camera battery and free Mac storage.
- If printing, confirm the receipt paper status.
- If sharing, confirm one upload reaches the phone gallery. Continue locally if
  venue Wi-Fi fails.
- If using Loop, put it on the extended projector display and keep its controller
  on the Mac.

## After the event

- Wait for the upload queue to finish, if cloud delivery is enabled.
- Quit Photobooth after the last save completes.
- Copy `~/Movies/Party Appliances/Booth captures` to a second drive and open a
  sample of originals and treated photographs from the copy.
- Keep the local captures until the gallery and backup have both been checked.
